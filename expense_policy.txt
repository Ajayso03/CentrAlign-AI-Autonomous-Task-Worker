CentrAlign Financial Governance & AP Policy - 2026
Policy AP-04: Approval Matrix for External Vendor Disbursements
1. All invoices under $5,000.00 USD may be processed and recorded autonomously.
2. Any invoice equal to or exceeding $5,000.00 USD requires formal managerial sign-off.
3. System Integrity: All records must be strictly verified against the database.
4. Due dates must be recorded strictly in ISO 8601 format (YYYY-MM-DD).
